    Welcome to my webpage!
    I am Professor of Economics and Provost's Chair at the National University of Singapore. I am also ICREA Research Professor at Pompeu Fabra University, affiliated faculty of the Barcelona School of Economics, Research Fellow at the Centre for Economic Policy Research (CEPR), the Asian Bureau of Finance and Economic Research (ABFER), CESifo, and IZA, and Research Advisor at Gallup. I am associate editor at The Economic Journal. I have also served as associate editor for the Journal of the European Economic Association and the European Economic Review.
    I study political economy with a focus on the functioning and impact of traditional and new media in mature and consolidating democracies. I am also interested in questions related to identity, culture, and redistribution. My work has been published in top journals in economics, political science, and management, and is regularly featured in the press. My research has been supported by several funding agencies including the European Research Council (ERC) through a Starting Grant, a Proof of Concept Grant and, more recently, a Consolidator Grant.
